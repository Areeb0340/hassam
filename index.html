More About
Hassam

I'm a creative video editor and animator with over 5 years of experience. I'm passionate about visual storytelling and dedicated to crafting polished, engaging content across video, motion, and design that connects with audiences.

I bring stories to life through the rhythm of editing and the energy of motion. My approach is intuitive, shaped by years of hands-on experience across editing, animation, and design.

I believe great work isn't just about visuals it's about emotion, timing, and connection, whether it's a 15-second edit or a full 3D scene.
